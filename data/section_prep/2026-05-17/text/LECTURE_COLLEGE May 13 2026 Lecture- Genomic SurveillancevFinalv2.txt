Benefits of Genomic Surveillance
1. Early detection and outbreak tracking
• Catching people before they interact with too many
others
• Identifying who has been exposed to an infected person
2. Vaccine and diagnostic development
• Can help identify pathogens to target in vaccines
3. Tracking antimicrobial resistance (AMR)
• Earlier detection of dangerous resistant strains
4. The more data collected, the more patterns become visible.
• Reducing uncertainty increases predictive power.
--- page 11 ---
Benefits of Genomic Surveillance
Informing public health policy
• Guide decisions regarding the introduction of
pharmacological and non-pharmacological interventions
• Has the promise of overcoming inequalities in access to
infectious disease diagnostics and control interventions
• An important tool for early detection of novel and endemic
pathogens in low-resource settings (low-resource settings
that are disproportionately affected by outbreak risk)
Genomic surveillance as a global public good!
